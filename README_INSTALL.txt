JARVIS VOICE ASSISTANT - APK KAISE BANWAYEN (5-10 minute, 100% free)

STEP 1: github.com par jayen, account banayen (free).
STEP 2: "New repository" banayen, naam kuch bhi rakhen (jaise jarvis).
STEP 3: "Add file" -> "Upload files" par jayen aur is ZIP ke saare
        files/folders waise hi upload karen (structure same rahe).
STEP 4: Green "Commit changes" button dabayen.
STEP 5: Upar "Actions" tab par jayen. "Build Jarvis APK" run ho raha
        hoga. 5-10 minute wait karen (green tick aayega).
STEP 6: Run par click karen -> "Artifacts" mein "jarvis-assistant-apk"
        milega. Download karen. Andar app-debug.apk hoga.
STEP 7: Phone mein APK transfer karen, open karen, "Install anyway /
        unknown sources allow" karen.
STEP 8: App kholein, microphone permission dein, button dabayen aur
        bolen, masalan:
          - "Kya time hai"
          - "WhatsApp kholo"
          - "YouTube kholo"
          - "Camera kholo"
          - "Call karo" (dialer kholta hai)
          - "Search karo islamabad weather"
          - "Tum kaun ho"

NOTE: Speech recognition ke liye phone mein Google app honi chahiye
(sab phones mein hoti hai). Agar Urdu/Roman Urdu sahi na sunay to
MainActivity.kt mein "ur-PK" ki jagah "hi-IN" ya "en-IN" likh den.
